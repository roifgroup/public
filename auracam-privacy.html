AuraCam Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

AuraCam is a baby-monitor app for Android. It turns your phone into a camera that streams live video and audio directly to a browser on another device connected to the same local network (your home WiFi, or the phone's own hotspot) using WebRTC. This policy explains what data AuraCam collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

Summary

  • Your camera and microphone feed is streamed directly, peer-to-peer, over your local network to the browser(s) you connect. It does not pass through AuraCam's developer or any server the developer operates.
  • AuraCam has no user accounts, no advertising, and does not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • If you turn on optional cloud recording, video is uploaded to a storage bucket you configure and control (e.g. your own Cloudflare R2, AWS S3, or similar account) — not to a server operated by AuraCam's developer.
  • If you leave optional diagnostics on, crash reports and error logs are sent to Sentry, a third-party crash-reporting service, to help fix bugs. Video, audio, your pairing PIN, and cloud storage credentials are never included.

Camera, microphone, and live video/audio

AuraCam requests camera and microphone access to capture the live stream. This audio/video is sent using WebRTC directly to whichever browser(s) you deliberately connect on your local network, protected by a 6-digit pairing PIN and an HTTPS/WSS-encrypted connection. It is not uploaded to, or stored by, any server operated by AuraCam's developer, and the developer has no access to it. Talk-back audio you speak into a connected browser is likewise sent directly to the phone for local playback only.

Recording

ModeWhat happens to the data
Local recording (optional) Video is written to a rolling buffer in the app's private storage on your own phone. It is only ever transferred off the phone when you tap "Download" in a connected browser, at which point it goes directly to that device, and nowhere else.
Cloud recording (optional) Video segments are uploaded directly from your phone to an S3-compatible storage bucket you set up and provide credentials for (Cloudflare R2, AWS S3, Backblaze B2, Wasabi, DigitalOcean Spaces, or a self-hosted endpoint). This is your own storage account, governed by that provider's own privacy policy and your own access controls — AuraCam's developer has no access to it. Your access-key credentials are encrypted on-device (Android Keystore) and are never transmitted anywhere except directly to the storage provider you chose, as part of the upload itself.

Diagnostics (optional, on by default, can be turned off in Settings)

AuraCam can send crash reports and error-level logs to Sentry, a third-party crash-reporting service, to help the developer find and fix bugs. When enabled, this may include:

  • Automatic crash/error reports (stack traces, the app version, and a curated set of internal error messages).
  • Basic device information: manufacturer, model, Android version, and app version.
  • If a stream happens to be running: non-identifying settings state such as selected video quality, frame rate, night mode, zoom level, and motion-detection sensitivity.
  • If you use the in-app "Report a bug" feature: the description you type, plus the device/settings information above.

Diagnostics data is filtered to exclude your pairing PIN, cloud storage credentials, and any video or audio content. This filtering happens automatically before anything is sent, including text you type into "Report a bug." Diagnostics can be turned off at any time in Settings, with no impact on the app's core functionality.

Permissions AuraCam requests, and why

  • Camera / Microphone — to capture the live stream and enable talk-back audio.
  • Notifications — used only if you enable motion-detection alerts.
  • WiFi / network state — used to determine your phone's local network address so it can be reached by a browser, and to detect whether the phone is on a metered (e.g. cellular hotspot) connection before allowing cloud uploads.
  • Local network access — AuraCam's core function is hosting a local web server that browsers on the same network connect to.

None of the above is used to identify you personally, build an advertising profile, or track you across other apps or websites.

What AuraCam does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or personal profile.
  • No advertising, ad SDKs, or advertising identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No location tracking.
  • No server operated by the developer that your video, audio, or recordings pass through.

Children's privacy

AuraCam is a tool operated by a parent or caregiver to monitor their own household; it is not directed at children, and it is not intended to be used directly by a child. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and your choices

  • Live video/audio is not stored anywhere by the app itself — it exists only for the duration of an active stream.
  • Local recordings are automatically overwritten as the rolling buffer fills, or you can uninstall the app to remove them immediately.
  • Cloud recordings are retained according to your own storage provider account and settings — you control deletion there.
  • You can turn off Diagnostics at any time in Settings; future events stop immediately. Existing events already sent are retained by Sentry per its own data-retention policy.

Security

The local connection between your phone and a viewer's browser is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS) and protected by a PIN you control. Cloud storage credentials are encrypted at rest on your device. Because AuraCam is designed to work entirely on your local network, video and audio never need to leave it unless you explicitly opt into cloud recording.
